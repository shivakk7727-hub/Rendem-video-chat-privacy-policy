Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application Random Video Chat.

App Name: Random Video Chat Package: com.aistudio.randomvideochat.fyrqlx Effective Date: July 27, 2026

🔒 Summary: We prioritize user privacy and safety. Live video/audio streams are transmitted directly peer-to-peer and are NOT recorded or stored on any server.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Random Video Chat, we may collect and process limited information necessary to deliver video chat functionality, optimize app performance, and show advertisements:

  • Camera and Microphone Input: Required solely to transmit real-time video and audio streams during active 1-on-1 video call sessions. This data is transmitted peer-to-peer via WebRTC and is never recorded, saved, or monitored on any permanent storage.
  • Device Information & Identifiers: Advertising ID (GAID), Android OS version, device model, and system diagnostics collected automatically by third-party services (such as Google AdMob and Firebase).
  • User Profile Info (Nickname / Gender / Country): Basic temporary details you provide when starting a chat match. This information is used strictly during session matching and is not shared or sold.
  • User Safety & Moderation Data: User report flags and blocked user IDs stored locally on your device to ensure you do not reconnect with blocked users.

2. Device Permissions Required

To provide full functionality, the application requests the following Android permissions:

  • android.permission.CAMERA - Required to capture video for live calls.
  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO - Required to capture audio for live calls.
  • android.permission.INTERNET - Required for peer-to-peer WebRTC signaling and loading advertisement content.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE - Used to verify internet connectivity.

3. Real-Time Communication & WebRTC

Video and audio connections are established using WebRTC technology and Firebase signaling servers:

  • No video or audio call sessions are recorded or stored on our servers or any third-party server.
  • Signaling data (IP addresses and connection tokens required to set up WebRTC peer connections) is temporarily processed in memory and discarded upon session completion.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements (Google AdMob)

We use third-party services to support app operations and monetize through advertisements. These third-party services may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Used to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to serve personalized or non-personalized ads according to your system settings.
  • Firebase Realtime Database & Analytics: Used for real-time signaling and connection routing between video chat participants.

For more details on Google's data practices, please visit Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Policies.

5. User Safety, Content Moderation & Conduct

We strictly prohibit any illegal, abusive, obscene, or adult content (UGC) during live video chats:

  • Users must be 18 years of age or older to use this application.
  • Nudity, sexual content, harassment, hate speech, violence, and illegal activity are strictly prohibited.
  • Block & Report Feature: Users can instantly report or block abusive participants at any time during a call. Blocked users will not be matched with you again.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard signaling data and protect user connections. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 18 ("Children"). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and realize your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can manage camera/microphone permissions anytime via your Android Device Settings (Settings > Apps > Random Video Chat > Permissions). Since we do not maintain persistent user accounts or store video recordings, no personal media accounts exist to delete.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted.
