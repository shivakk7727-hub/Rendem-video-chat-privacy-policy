Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

App Name: Random Video Chat  |  Effective Date: August 9, 2026

Welcome to Random Video Chat ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting user data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information, audio/video streams, and user data requests.

1. Age Requirement (18+ Only)

This application is strictly intended for users aged 18 and older. Minors (under 18) are strictly prohibited from using Random Video Chat.

2. Peer-to-Peer Video Chat (WebRTC)

Random Video Chat uses WebRTC technology to connect users directly peer-to-peer (P2P) for real-time video and audio conversations.

  • No Recording or Storage: We do NOT record, save, store, or monitor your live video or audio streams on any server.
  • Direct Connection: Your video and audio transmission occurs directly between your device and the connected user's device.

3. Information We Collect and App Permissions

To deliver real-time video chat functionality, the app requests access to the following device features:

  • Camera Permission: Required solely to capture live video stream during an active chat session.
  • Microphone Permission: Required solely to transmit audio during live chat sessions.
  • Network State & Internet: Required to establish peer-to-peer socket connections and display third-party advertisements.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

Our app utilizes trusted third-party service providers that may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: Used for app stability, temporary signaling, and performance monitoring.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (advertising ID) to serve relevant ads.

5. Account Creation and Data Deletion Policy

Data Deletion Request & Rights:

Random Video Chat does NOT require account registration or store permanent user profiles on servers. All video sessions and chat metadata are transient (temporary) and erased immediately when a call ends or when you exit the app.

If you have communicated with us via email or believe any temporary technical log or feedback data remains, you have the right to request complete deletion of your data.

How to request data deletion:

  • Send an email to shivakk7727@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • Include any details regarding your request. We will review and process your request within 48 hours and permanently delete any associated records.

6. Security

We prioritize the security of our users. Since video calls are transmitted directly via encrypted WebRTC connections and no personal data is stored on external databases, your interactions remain private.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
