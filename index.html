Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Random Video Chat App

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to Random Video Chat. Your privacy is very important to us. This page explains our policy regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our service.

1. Information Collection & Data Storage

Our app provides anonymous peer-to-peer random video chatting. We strive to collect as little personal data as possible:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal details (such as name, phone number, or password) to use the app.
  • No Data Retention: Video and audio streams are processed in real-time between devices via WebRTC and are NEVER stored or recorded on any server.
  • Local Settings: App preferences (such as camera or audio settings) are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to external databases.

2. Data Deletion Policy & Request

Data Deletion Request: Since our app does not store any user accounts, personal profiles, or personal data on remote servers, there is no remote account data to delete.

If you wish to remove all local data associated with the app from your device:

  • Simply clear the app storage/cache from your phone settings or uninstall the app. All local settings will be permanently erased immediately.
  • If you have any questions or wish to submit a data deletion inquiry, you can email us directly at: shivakk7727@gmail.com.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, such as Google Play Services or AdMob for displaying ads. These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Permissions

To provide video chatting features, the app requests camera and microphone permissions. These permissions are strictly used for real-time video/audio streaming and are never used to record or save media.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
